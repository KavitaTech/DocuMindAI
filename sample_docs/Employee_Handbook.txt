Annual Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave
per calendar year. Employees should submit planned leave
requests at least 5 working days in advance.

Expense Reimbursement
Employees must submit reimbursement claims within
30 calendar days of the expense. Approved claims are
processed within 7 working days.

Notice Period
The standard employee resignation notice period is
30 calendar days unless an employment agreement states otherwise.
